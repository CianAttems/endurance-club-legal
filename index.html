Endurance Club

Privacy Policy

Effective 13 September 2026 Beta / testing release App version 0.1.0
Plain-language summary
  • Endurance Club is a race calendar app. You don't need an account, email, or password to use it.
  • Your preferences (liked races, home location, unit setting) are stored on your device, not in a personal account on our servers.
  • If you enter a home postcode, it's converted to map coordinates so we can show you nearby races and sort by distance — that lookup goes to OpenStreetMap, a nonprofit mapping service, and to our database when you search.
  • We don't run ads, sell data, or track you across other apps or websites.

01Who this covers

This policy describes what Endurance Club ("the app," "we") collects and how it's used, for anyone installing it as part of testing or early access. It's written for a small beta group ahead of a public release, and we'll update it as the app grows — see the changes section below.

02What we collect

InformationWhere it's storedWhy
Home postcode/city and country, and the map coordinates we derive from it On your device To sort races by distance and show a "near you" view
Liked and attending races, sport interests, distance unit (km/mi) On your device To remember your preferences between sessions
A randomly generated installation ID (not tied to your name or email) On your device; shared with our database only if you send or use an invite link To record which install invited another, for a future friend-connect feature
Which races you "like" Sent to our database, without any personal identifier attached To prioritize which races we research and add full details for

We do not ask for your name, email address, or a password to use the app. There is currently no user account system — the app works entirely from the data on your own device, plus race data it reads from our database.

03Who we share it with

  • Supabase (our database host) stores the race calendar itself and receives your search/filter criteria — including your coordinates, if you've set a home location and search by distance — to return matching results. It does not receive your name or email.
  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim, a nonprofit geocoding service, receives the postcode or city text you enter, in order to return map coordinates. We don't send them anything else about you.
  • Expo/EAS, our app-hosting infrastructure, delivers app updates and may collect standard, non-identifying technical diagnostics (crash logs, device type) the same way most mobile apps built on this infrastructure do.

We do not sell any information, and we do not share it with data brokers or advertisers.

04What we don't do

—No advertising or ad-tracking SDKs are included in this build.
—No location tracking in the background — your coordinates are only used when the app is open and you've set or used a home location.
—No cross-app or cross-site tracking, and no data sold to third parties.
—No account, password, or real-name signup required to use the app.

05Your control over your data

Since most of your data lives on your device, clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes your preferences, liked races, and home location entirely. If you'd like the race-like signals or referral record tied to your installation ID removed from our database, email us at the address below and we'll delete them.

06Children's privacy

Endurance Club is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has used the app and provided information to us, contact us and we'll remove it.

07Changes to this policy

As the app moves from testing into public release — adding accounts, a friends system, or (later) advertising — this policy will be updated to reflect it, and the effective date above will change. Testers will be notified of material changes.
