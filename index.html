The Endurance Club

Privacy Policy

Effective 20 October 2026 Beta / testing release App version 0.1.2
Plain-language summary
  • The Endurance Club is a race calendar and community app. You create an account with your email and a password.
  • We store your profile (name, date of birth, gender, home location, sports), your liked and attended races, your friends and your chat messages on our servers, so they follow you between devices.
  • Your date of birth is never shown to other users. Friends see your name, photo, home city and the races you attend.
  • You can delete your account and all its data yourself, inside the app: Profile, then Delete account.
  • We do not run ads, sell data, or track you across other apps or websites. The app is for people aged 18 and over.

01Who we are

The Endurance Club ("the app," "we") is operated by Cian Attems, Vienna, Austria, who is the data controller under the GDPR. Contact: attemsphotography@gmail.com. This is a beta release for a small group of testers ahead of a public launch, and the policy will be updated as the app grows.

02What we collect

InformationWhy we need it
Email address and password (the password is stored only as a hash)To create your account and sign you in
NameShown to friends and in search, so people can find and recognise you
Date of birth and gender (both required)To confirm you are 18 or older, and to give clubs anonymous age and gender statistics. Never shown to other users.
Home postcode, city, country and the map coordinates derived from them; distance unitTo show distances to races and filter by distance from home
Sports and destination countries you pickTo personalise your race list and recommendations

Friend requests and connectionsTo connect you with training partners
Chat messages (direct, group and club chats) and message reports you sendTo deliver messages, and to moderate abuse
Push notification tokenTo send notifications you allow, such as messages and friend requests
A private calendar-feed tokenTo let you subscribe to your attended races in your phone calendar
Crash logs, diagnostics and app performance dataTo find and fix bugs and keep the app fast and stable

We derive your approximate home location only from the postcode or city you type in. The app does not read your phone's GPS and does not track your location in the background.

03Who can see what

  • Other members who search for you can see your name, profile photo and home city and country.
  • Accepted friends can also see the races you attend and like, and can chat with you.
  • Club organisers only see anonymous, aggregated statistics (for example age groups), never an individual's date of birth.
  • Nobody else can see your email address, date of birth, gender or exact coordinates.

04Who we share it with

  • Supabase hosts our database, authentication and file storage. It processes your data on our behalf.
  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim, a nonprofit geocoding service, receives the postcode or city text you enter, to return map coordinates. Nothing else about you is sent.
  • Expo delivers push notifications and app updates, and receives your push token and basic device information.
  • Sentry receives crash logs, diagnostics and performance data.
  • Hotel booking links (Stay22): if you open a hotel search from a race page, you leave the app and that provider's own privacy policy applies. We may earn a commission on bookings. We do not send them your profile.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with data brokers or advertisers.

05What we do not do

—No advertising or ad-tracking SDKs.
—No background location tracking and no GPS access.
—No cross-app or cross-site tracking, and no data sold to third parties.
—No health, fitness-tracker or financial data collected.

06How long we keep it, and deleting your account

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time inside the app (Profile, then Delete account). Deletion permanently removes your account, profile, liked and attended races, reviews, friend connections, chat messages and push tokens. You can also request deletion by email, or see the steps on our delete account page. Encrypted backups are overwritten within 90 days.

07Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can edit your profile in the app, and you can contact us for anything else. You may also complain to the Austrian Data Protection Authority (dsb.gv.at). We process your data to run the app you asked to use (contract), to keep it secure and moderated (legitimate interest), and, where you opt in, to send notifications (consent).

08Age limit

The Endurance Club is for people aged 18 and over. We ask for your date of birth when you sign up and do not allow accounts for anyone younger. If you believe a minor has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

09Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), passwords are stored hashed, and access to other members' data is restricted by database access rules.

10Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect, for example by adding advertising, we will update this policy and the effective date above, and notify users of material changes.

11Contact

Questions, access or deletion requests, or feedback: attemsphotography@gmail.com.
