Delete your account

This page explains how to delete your account and data for The Endurance Club (developer: Cian Attems).

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to attemsphotography@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Endurance Club account".
  2. Write from the email address you registered with, so we can verify the account is yours.
  3. We delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account and login (email address, password hash)
  • Your profile, attended and liked races, and friend connections
  • Your chat messages and push notification tokens

What is kept

Nothing is kept after deletion, apart from routine encrypted backups, which are overwritten within 90 days.

See also our privacy policy.
